Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026  ·  Effective: 26 November 2026

Back to Home

Balbhadra Solutions ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our clients, website visitors, and business contacts. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and share it, and your rights in relation to it. Our services are classified under NIC 62020 — Computer consultancy and computer facilities management activities.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Contact Information: name, work email address, phone number, job title, and company name when you fill in a contact or enquiry form.
  • Communication Data: emails, meeting notes, and messages you send us during the course of a business relationship or enquiry.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, referring URLs, and pages visited, collected automatically via server logs or analytics tools.
  • Contract & Billing Data: information necessary to perform a service agreement, raise invoices, or fulfil regulatory obligations.
  • Sensitive / Special Category Data: we do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data. If you share any in the course of a project engagement, it will be handled with additional safeguards.

2. How We Collect Information

  • Directly from you via web forms, email, phone calls, or in-person meetings.
  • Automatically through cookies, server logs, and analytics on this website.
  • From third parties such as business referrals, LinkedIn, or public business directories.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information under one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Contract: processing is necessary to perform a contract with you or take pre-contractual steps.
  • Legitimate Interests: to run our business, improve our services, and communicate with existing or prospective clients.
  • Legal Obligation: to comply with applicable laws, regulations, and court orders.
  • Consent: where you have given us explicit permission (e.g., subscribing to newsletters). You may withdraw consent at any time.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotations or proposals.
  • To deliver contracted IT consultancy and facilities management services.
  • To manage client accounts, invoicing, and payments.
  • To send service-related communications and updates.
  • To improve and secure our website and IT systems.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements.
  • To send marketing communications where you have opted in (you may opt out at any time).

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with:

  • Service Providers: trusted third-party vendors (cloud hosting, accounting software, email providers) under data-processing agreements.
  • Professional Advisors: lawyers, auditors, and insurers where necessary.
  • Regulatory Authorities: government bodies or law enforcement where required by law.
  • Business Transfers: in the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction.

6. Cookies

This website uses essential cookies required for site functionality and, where consent is given, analytics cookies to understand how visitors use the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law. Client data is typically retained for seven (7) years after the end of a contract to meet statutory accounting and audit requirements. Website enquiry data is retained for up to two (2) years.

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, destruction, or alteration. These include encryption in transit (TLS), access controls, and regular security assessments aligned with ISO 27001 practices.

9. International Transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside India or the EEA, we ensure adequate safeguards are in place through Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms recognised under applicable data protection law.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request erasure of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict or object to certain processing activities.
  • Request portability of your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (without affecting prior lawful processing).
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 12.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are directed at businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under the age of 18. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete the data promptly.
